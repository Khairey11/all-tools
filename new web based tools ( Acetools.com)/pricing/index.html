Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

Are my files secure?

Yes. All processing happens entirely within your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or seen by anyone else. We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all connections.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts or hidden fees. You can cancel your Pro or Business subscription at any time right from your account dashboard.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) as well as PayPal and Apple Pay for secure, fast transactions.

Is there a free trial?

We don't offer a time-limited free trial because our Free plan is available forever! You can use the core tools 3 times a day at zero cost to test our quality before upgrading.
